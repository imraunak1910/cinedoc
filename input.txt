Rajkumar Hirani directed 3 Idiots, a 2009 Hindi-language comedy-drama starring Aamir Khan, R. Madhavan, Sharman Joshi, Kareena Kapoor and Boman Irani. The film follows three engineering students at an Indian college and explores friendship, education, pressure and following one's passion. The movie was produced by Vidhu Vinod Chopra and received several awards.
